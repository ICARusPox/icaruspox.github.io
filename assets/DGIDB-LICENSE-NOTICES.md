## 2. Licensing Model & Terms of Use

DGIdb is an open-access research resource maintained by the Griffith Lab (Washington University School of Medicine). 

The DGIdb codebase and database schema are open-source. However, DGIdb aggregates data from more than 40 primary source databases, each governed by its respective provider license and terms of use.
